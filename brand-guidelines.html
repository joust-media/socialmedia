01 — Typography

Four Families, Fixed Jobs

Inter is the product typeface — all UI, dashboards, and tables. Open Sans is for email only, chosen as a web-safe fallback that renders reliably in every client. Poppins carries marketing heroes; Orbitron is a rare accent. Bold is the only emphasis — italics are rare. All faces load free from Google Fonts.

Email Only — Open Sans

04 — Iconography

Stroke-Based, 2px, Rounded

07 — Framework

How Layouts Hold Together

Emails and marketing lock to a 650px canvas with 32px horizontal padding. Vertical rhythm runs 16 / 32 / 48. Borders do the work of elevation; shadows stay at icon scale.

Captions Guide
Acxiom sold your data. We got it deleted.
  • Near-black pill (#121212, 14px radius) on footage — white Poppins SemiBold, centered, one line where possible
  • ALL CAPS, 1–2 short sentences. Concrete and direct — name the broker, state the result
  • Highlight the key word in Privacy Yellow (#FCB223) — one word per caption
  • Caption sits in the lower third, always inside the safe zone, with the dark-mode logo below-left
  • Headlines (when used) run all-caps Poppins Bold, white, at the top of the content zone
Safe Zones
Feed posts (1:1, 4:5) — keep everything 60px in from every edge.
Stories & Reels (9:16) — 270px clear at top, 380–670px at bottom for platform UI, 65px at the sides.
TikTok & Shorts (9:16) — 150px clear at top, 320–380px at bottom, 140–150px on the right for action rail.
Post-roll sting — every video ends on the brand close: logo settle + tagline on near-black.
